        🤝 Safe Zone

        Get medically reviewed answers to your common questions:

        • Q: What’s a normal cycle length?
          A: Between 21–35 days is considered normal.
        • Q: Can I shower during my period?
          A: Yes! Daily hygiene is important during menstruation.
        • Q: Is it okay to skip periods sometimes?
          A: Occasional changes are fine, but consistent skipping should be checked by a doctor.
        • Q: What are safe ways to reduce cramps?
          A: Heat pads, gentle exercise, hydration, and some yoga poses may help.
